What Ridni is

Ridni is an app for recording what happens with your family — sleep, feeds, diapers, growth, health notes, pregnancy and cycles — and sharing those records with the adults you invite. That is the whole service. It is provided by Andrii Sotnikov, Kyiv, Ukraine.

Who can use it

Adults. You need to be 18 or older to create an account. Ridni is for a parent or carer keeping a record; it is not addressed to children and we do not create accounts for them.

Your account

Use a real email address you can receive mail at — it is how you get back in, and how we confirm a request about your data is yours. Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else has access to your account, change it and tell us.

Your family, and what the roles mean

The person who creates a family owns it. They can invite parents, who can do everything except hand over ownership, and caregivers, who can see the children and add records but cannot change who is in the family. Anyone you invite will see the records in the family from then on, including ones added before they joined — invite accordingly.

Records show who added them. If you leave or delete your account, the records you added stay with the family without your name.

Ridni does not give medical advice

This matters more than anything else on this page, so it is not in small print:

Ridni is a record, not an assessment. It does not tell you whether a temperature is dangerous, whether a dose is safe, whether a measurement is a problem, or whether a vaccination is due. It has no medicine database and no vaccination schedule. The growth chart shows a WHO percentile band and nothing more. Nothing in the app is a diagnosis, a recommendation, or a substitute for a doctor — if you are worried about your child or yourself, ask a doctor, not an app.
