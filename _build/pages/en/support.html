Support

Effective 20 November 2026.

Write to support@ridni.app and a person answers. There is no ticket number and no bot. If your question is about your data — a copy of it, a correction, deletion — use the form at the bottom of this page, because those have a deadline we have to meet.

Questions people actually ask

How do I add my partner?

More → Family members → Invite someone, or the invite step during setup. You get a six-digit family code that is valid for seven days. Your partner installs Ridni, creates their own account, and enters the code. You both then see every feed, nap and diaper as it is recorded.

What can a caregiver see?

The children and their records, and they can add records too. A caregiver cannot change who is in the family or what anyone's role is, and cannot see cycle tracking. The role is set when you invite them and can be changed later by a parent.

Why doesn't the app tell me if something is normal?

Because it does not know, and a confident answer about your child from software that has never seen your child is worse than no answer. Ridni keeps the record and shows you the number. The growth chart draws the WHO percentile band so you can see where a measurement sits, and that is as far as it goes: no verdicts, no "you should", no schedule you are behind on.

Why is there no vaccination schedule?

Schedules differ by country and change, and a wrong one would be dangerous in a specific way: you would trust it. Ridni stores the vaccinations you enter, with the dates you enter. Nothing in it is due or overdue.

A timer is running and I cannot stop it.

Whoever started a timer can stop it from their phone; after thirty minutes the lock lifts and anyone can. This is deliberate — two adults stopping the same nap from two phones is how a sleep record ends up wrong. You can still add a note to a timer you did not start.

What happens when I have no connection?

Everything keeps working. Records are saved on the phone and sync when the connection comes back; a banner tells you how many are waiting. If the same thing was edited on two phones while one was offline, the app shows both versions and asks which to keep — it never picks for you. The version you do not keep is not stored, and only the field you chose changes; the rest of the record stays as it was.

I deleted something by mistake.

There is a five-second undo on the toast. After that it is gone.

How do I delete my account?

More → Account → Delete account, or ridni.app/delete-account if you cannot sign in. For seven days, signing in brings everything back. What happens to the records you added is explained on that page.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, by asking — this version has no export button. Use the form below; we answer within a month.

Can I use Ridni in my language?

The app follows your phone: English, Ukrainian, Russian, Polish, German, Spanish and French. You can change it in More → Region & units.

A data request

The form prepares an email to privacy@ridni.app in your mail app. Send it from the address on your account.

What happens next. You get a reply from a person within one month, usually sooner. If you asked for a copy, it comes as a file you can read without Ridni. If you asked for deletion, we tell you exactly what is deleted and what stays with your family — the same thing ridni.app/delete-account explains, so there is no surprise.

If we cannot match the address to an account we say so and ask nothing further; we do not confirm or deny that an address is registered to anyone who is not writing from it.
