Ridni

A shared log for your family. Sleep, feeds, diapers, growth and health notes, recorded once and seen by the adults you invite, and by nobody else.

What it does

  • Records sleep, feeds, diapers, pumping, growth, temperatures, medications, vaccinations, pregnancy and cycle entries.
  • Syncs them to your partner and caregivers, with a role for each person.
  • Keeps working without a connection and syncs when it comes back.
  • Available in English, Ukrainian, Russian, Polish, German, Spanish and French.

What it does not do

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • No medical advice. Ridni keeps the record; your doctor makes the assessments.

Questions? Write to support@ridni.app.
