Deleting your Ridni account

Effective 20 November 2026.

This page exists so you can delete your account without having to ask anyone, and because Google Play requires it to be reachable without signing in.

In the app

More → Account → Delete account. You will be asked to type DELETE, and then:

  • the account is scheduled, not erased. For seven days, signing in again restores everything exactly as it was;
  • after seven days the account is anonymised: your name is replaced, your email address is replaced with one that goes nowhere, your password is erased, your push token and notification settings are deleted, and a Google or Apple sign-in is disconnected. What is left is a record with nothing in it that identifies you;
  • the records you added stay with your family, shown as added by a former member, with no name attached. They are your family's log of their own children, and removing them would delete another parent's history along with yours;
  • your cycle entries are deleted — they were never visible to the rest of your family, so there is nothing to keep for them;
  • the push token for your phone is deleted, so no reminder can reach it.

If you are the only adult in the family, the app says so before you continue: there would be nobody left who can manage the children's profiles. You can hand ownership to someone else first (More → Family members → Transfer ownership), or confirm and let the family go with the account.

If you cannot sign in

Write to support@ridni.app from the address on the account, with the word delete in the message. We reply within one month, usually within a few days, and we tell you what was deleted. We will not act on a request from a different address — not to make it difficult, but because an account someone else can delete is not safe.

What we keep after deletion, and why

Nothing that identifies you. The account row stays so that the family's records still have something to hang on, with your name, email, password and tokens gone from it. The records themselves remain with the family and carry no name. If the last adult in a family deletes their account, the family's data goes with it — the app says so before you confirm.

Database backups are kept for up to seven days, so data removed from the live database can remain in a backup for at most seven more days.
